## Why Schools Choose MailMouse

### Educational Communication Challenges

Schools and educational institutions face unique communication requirements:

- **Privacy regulations** like GDPR require careful handling of student/family data
- **Mixed technology comfort** among parents and staff creates barriers
- **Multiple stakeholders** (teachers, parents, administrators) need different access levels
- **Google Groups** may scan content for ads, raising privacy concerns
- **Complex systems** require IT support that many schools lack

### MailMouse's Educational Advantage

**GDPR Compliance by Design**: Hosted in the EU with strict data protection standards. No email scanning, no ads, no data mining.

**Simple for Everyone**: Parents and teachers don't need special accounts or technical knowledge. It's just email.

**Controlled Communication**: Teachers and administrators control who can post, ensuring appropriate communication channels.
