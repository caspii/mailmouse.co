## Why MailMouse for Company Teams?

### The Problem with Current Solutions

Small businesses and startups face unique communication challenges:

- **Google Groups** requires everyone to have Google accounts and navigate confusing interfaces
- **Slack/Teams** can be overwhelming for simple announcements
- **Email chains** quickly become unmanageable with reply-all chaos
- **IT complexity** of setting up your own email server is too much for small teams

### How MailMouse Solves This

**Simple Team Lists**: Create dedicated mailing lists for different teams and purposes:
- `all-hands@yourcompany.com` for company-wide announcements
- `engineering@yourcompany.com` for technical discussions
- `sales@yourcompany.com` for sales team coordination
- `board@yourcompany.com` for executive communications

**Zero IT Overhead**: No servers to maintain, no software to install. Just create your list and start communicating.

**Works with Existing Email**: Your team can use their current email addresses - Gmail, Outlook, or company email. No need for everyone to sign up for yet another service.
