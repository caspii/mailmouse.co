## Why churches still run on email

Most congregations span three or four generations. The youth group lives in a chat app. Many older members only read email — and some will never install WhatsApp. Email is the one channel that reaches everyone.

The problem is managing it. Most church offices know these:

- **The giant BCC list.** Someone copies 200 addresses into every newsletter. Someone always gets left out.
- **The spam folder.** Members say "I never got it." Free list tools often land in junk, especially for Yahoo and work addresses.
- **The departing volunteer.** The administrator who set up the list moves on. Nobody else has the password.
- **The wrong reply-all.** A private prayer request goes to the whole congregation by mistake.

## How MailMouse helps

### One list for each purpose

Most churches need three to five lists. For example:

| List | Who posts | Who receives |
|---|---|---|
| `news@yourchurch.org` | Office and clergy only | Whole congregation |
| `prayer@yourchurch.org` | Any member | Prayer team |
| `volunteers@yourchurch.org` | Anyone on the list | Rota volunteers |
| `council@yourchurch.org` | Council members | Council members (private) |
| `youth-parents@yourchurch.org` | Youth leaders | Parents |

An **announcement list** lets only chosen people send. A **discussion list** lets everyone reply to everyone. You choose per list.

### Built to reach the inbox

MailMouse sends each message in a way that passes the sender checks Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo use. That keeps your Sunday notices out of the spam folder. [Why group email goes to spam](/why-group-emails-go-to-spam/).

### Survives changes in leadership

Every list has at least two owners — for example the parish administrator and a churchwarden. When someone steps down, they hand the list to their successor. Members, settings and message history stay in place.

### Private and respectful

Members never see each other's addresses. MailMouse is hosted in the EU, is GDPR compliant, shows no ads, and never scans your messages. Pastoral information stays within the church.

## Questions churches ask

**Do older members need to do anything?**
No. You add their email address. They receive messages like any other email and reply in the usual way.

**Can we use our church's own domain?**
Yes. Use an address like `news@stmarys.org`, or a free `@mailmouse.co` address.

**Can members leave on their own?**
Yes. Every message has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also add or remove members yourself.

**We have a list in Google Groups. Can we move it?**
Yes. Export your members and upload them to MailMouse. See our [Google Groups alternative](/google-groups-alternative/) page.
