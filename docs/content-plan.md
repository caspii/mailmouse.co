# Content plan — decided 2026-11-26

Based on [research/2026-11-market-research.md](research/2026-11-market-research.md).

## Positioning decision

**Primary audience:** volunteer-run groups (churches, residents' associations,
clubs, schools' parent groups, non-profits). Small companies stay as a
secondary audience.

**Core promise (three pillars, in this order):**
1. **Lands in the inbox.** We handle the technical sender checks that send
   Google Groups mail to spam.
2. **Outlives the organiser.** Co-owners and handover, so the list survives
   when the secretary steps down.
3. **One flat price per group.** Never per member. Free for small groups.

Supporting points (kept, but demoted): any email provider, no member accounts,
EU-hosted, no ads.

**Headline:** "Group email that reaches everyone — and keeps working when the
organiser moves on."

## Rules for all new copy

- Pre-launch: describe features as "MailMouse will…" or "built to…". Never
  imply something works today.
- No invented testimonials. The existing use-case pages contain illustrative
  quotes; replace them with real ones after launch.
- No member counts or social proof numbers unless verified.
