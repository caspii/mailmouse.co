## Why Researchers Need Better Communication

### The Challenge of Multi-Institutional Collaboration

Research teams face unique communication challenges that traditional tools don't address:

- **Distributed teams** across multiple universities and time zones
- **Institutional email restrictions** that limit external collaboration
- **Project continuity** when team members change institutions
- **Knowledge preservation** as researchers graduate or move positions
- **Mixed technology environments** with different IT policies
- **Funding documentation** requiring persistent communication records

### How MailMouse Serves Research

**Institution-Independent**: Works with any university email system worldwide. No need for everyone to be on the same platform.

**Project Persistence**: Communications stay with the project, not the institution. When researchers move, the collaboration continues.

**Simple Archives**: Keep track of important discussions and decisions. Perfect for documenting research progress and maintaining institutional memory.
